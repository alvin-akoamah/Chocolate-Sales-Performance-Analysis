
# Retail Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI

### An End-to-End Data Analytics Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end retail sales performance analysis using **Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI, and DAX**.

The objective was to transform raw retail transaction data into an **interactive, insight-driven Power BI dashboard** that helps stakeholders understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and explore customer purchasing behaviour.

The analysis covers **2023–2024** and features six interactive report pages examining different aspects of retail business performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Initial data inspection and preparation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and validation
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, visualization, and dashboard development
- **DAX** – KPI calculations, business measures, and time-intelligence analysis

## Key Findings & Insights

- **Total Revenue:** 25.24M
- **Total Profit:** 10.10M
- **Profit Margin:** 40%
- **Total Orders:** Approximately 990K
- **Total Customers:** Approximately 50K
- **Year-over-Year Revenue Growth:** +0.12% (2024 vs. 2023)
- **Most Profitable Category:** Praline, generating approximately 2.7M in profit
- **Highest-Revenue Country:** Australia, contributing approximately 6.32M in revenue
- **Top Product by Revenue:** Dark Chocolate 50%, generating approximately 1.78M
- **Discount Performance:** Products sold without discounts contributed the largest share of revenue, while profit margins remained at 40% across all discount bands.

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report consists of **six interactive analytical pages**:

1. **Executive Summary** – Overview of revenue, profit, profit margin, orders, customers, and YoY growth.
2. **Product Performance** – Analysis of product categories, brands, best-selling products, and profitability.
3. **Store & Geography** – Comparison of revenue and profit across countries, cities, store types, and individual stores.
4. **Customer Insights** – Exploration of customer demographics, loyalty membership, and purchasing behaviour.
5. **Time Analysis** – Examination of monthly and yearly revenue trends and growth patterns.
6. **Discount Analysis** – Evaluation of sales volume, revenue, profit, and profit margins across discount bands.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

The analysis identified several findings that can support business decision-making:

- **Revenue Stability:** Revenue increased by 0.12% from 2023 to 2024, indicating relatively stable business performance.
- **Product Performance:** Praline was the most profitable category, suggesting an opportunity to review inventory availability and merchandising strategies for high-performing products.
- **Geographic Performance:** Australia generated the highest overall revenue, highlighting an important market for continued performance monitoring.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer demographics and loyalty segmentation provide opportunities to further investigate purchasing patterns and customer engagement.
- **Discount Strategy:** Non-discounted transactions generated the highest revenue, while reported profit margins remained consistent across discount levels. Further analysis would be needed to determine the effectiveness of discount promotions.

## Analysis Limitations

- The dataset covers only **two years (2023–2024)**, limiting conclusions about long-term trends and recurring seasonality.
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causal relationships between discounts, customer characteristics, and sales performance.
- Profitability calculations are based on the cost and revenue information available in the dataset.

## Project Takeaway

This project demonstrates practical skills in **data cleaning, transformation, dimensional data modeling, DAX calculations, exploratory analysis, and interactive dashboard development**.

By converting transaction-level data into meaningful business insights, the dashboard provides a structured way to monitor retail sales performance, evaluate profitability, and identify areas for further business investigation.
